# The Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and everything bound to it by gravity. It has eight planets. The four inner planets, Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars, are small and rocky. The four outer planets, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune, are much larger; Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants, while Uranus and Neptune are ice giants.

Jupiter is the largest planet and has a giant storm called the Great Red Spot. Saturn is famous for its bright rings made of ice and rock. Mars appears red because of iron oxide on its surface. Venus is the hottest planet because its thick carbon dioxide atmosphere traps heat.

Between Mars and Jupiter lies the asteroid belt. Beyond Neptune is the Kuiper Belt, home to dwarf planets such as Pluto. The Sun contains about 99.8 percent of the total mass of the Solar System.
